# YourBrand

A modern, responsive multi-page corporate and business consulting website built with **React**, **Vite**, and **Tailwind CSS**. **YourBrand** showcases strategic consulting, technology solutions, creative design, sustainability initiatives, and client resources through a clean, accessible interface.

🔗 **Live Demo:** [react2-three-chi.vercel.app](https://react2-three-chi.vercel.app/)

---

## ✨ Features

- **Multi-Page Navigation:** Seamless client-side routing across **Home**, **About**, **Services**, **Resources**, and **Contact** pages with a persistent header and "Get Started" CTA.
- **Home Showcase:** Highlights core value propositions (*Sustainable Growth*, *Trusted Services*, *Expert Team*, *24/7 Support*), strategic priorities, and service overviews.
- **Company Mission & Values (`/about`):** Presents the organization's mission, vision, and foundational pillars (*Trust*, *Creativity*, *Collaboration*, *Excellence*).
- **Service Catalog (`/services`):** Structured breakdown of 6 core offerings:
  - Business Consulting
  - Technology Solutions
  - Business Growth
  - Creative Design
  - Sustainability
  - Customer Support
- **Resource Hub (`/resources`):** Curated guides and toolkits including the *Getting Started Guide*, *Business Insights*, *Sustainability Guide*, and *Innovation Toolkit*.
- **Interactive Contact Page (`/contact`):** Clean inquiry form (Full Name, Email Address, Subject, Message) paired with social links.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React, JavaScript (ES6+), HTML5, CSS3
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (`@tailwindcss/vite`)
- **Build Tool & Linter:** Vite, ESLint
- **Deployment:** Vercel
